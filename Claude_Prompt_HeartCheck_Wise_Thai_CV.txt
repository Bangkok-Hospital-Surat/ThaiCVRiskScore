You are continuing an existing clinical web-app project named “HeartCheck Wise”.

Your task is to develop a new Thai-first module:

HEARTCHECK WISE – THAI CV RISK SCORE (NON-LAB)

Primary objective:
Build a safe, auditable, client-side online Thai cardiovascular risk assessment that does NOT require laboratory values and provides plain-language interpretation similar in usability to the existing PREVENT experience.

CRITICAL RULE:
DO NOT start by inventing or hard-coding the Thai CV Risk Score equation.

PHASE 1 MUST BE RESEARCH + VERIFICATION.

1. Identify the official or best-supported Thai CV Risk Score NON-LAB equation.
Prioritize sources in this order:
- Original derivation paper / Thai cohort publication
- Official Thai CV Risk calculator implementation
- Ramathibodi / Mahidol source or implementation
- Thai Ministry of Public Health
- Royal College of Physicians of Thailand guidelines
- Peer-reviewed Thai validation publications

2. Cross-check the mathematical implementation against at least TWO independent authoritative sources.

3. Document explicitly:
- eligible age range
- primary-prevention inclusion/exclusion
- outcome definition
- prediction horizon
- sex handling
- age term(s)
- SBP term(s)
- diabetes coding
- smoking coding
- height
- waist circumference
- waist-to-height ratio if applicable
- transformations
- interaction terms
- coefficients
- baseline survival / intercept
- rounding behavior
- units
- male/female differences

4. If the official equation cannot be verified with sufficient confidence:
STOP.
Do not implement a calculator.
Produce a verification-gap report describing exactly what cannot be confirmed and what source is still required.

5. If verified, build a GOLDEN VALIDATION SET before UI development.
Create at least 20–50 synthetic cases covering:
- male/female
- low/middle/high age
- smoker/non-smoker
- diabetes/no diabetes
- low/medium/high SBP
- low/high waist circumference
- multiple waist-height ratios
- low/moderate/high predicted risk

For every case record:
- all inputs
- expected risk
- reference implementation/source
- calculated risk
- absolute error
- PASS/FAIL

Use a tolerance consistent with the precision of the official/reference calculator.

NO PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT until all validation cases pass.

6. Build the architecture with strict separation:

/src
  /calculator
    thaiCvNonLab.js
    validationCases.js

  /eligibility
    eligibilityRules.js

  /interpretation
    riskInterpretation.js
    recommendationRules.js

  /config
    riskThresholds.js
    references.js

  /components
    InputForm
    ResultCard
    RiskDrivers
    RecommendationCard
    Disclaimer

The equation engine must be independent from the interpretation engine and UI.

7. Core inputs expected for the non-lab pathway:
- age
- sex
- current smoking
- diabetes
- systolic blood pressure
- height
- waist circumference

Do not assume this list is final until the verified equation confirms it.

8. Eligibility / safety gate:
This is a PRIMARY PREVENTION risk tool.

Users with established ASCVD/CVD should not receive a primary-prevention risk percentage.

Screen for at least:
- previous myocardial infarction
- known coronary artery disease
- previous stroke/TIA
- peripheral arterial disease
- PCI/CABG or other coronary revascularization
- other established ASCVD as supported by the source model/guideline

For ineligible users, show a clear Thai message explaining that the tool is not designed for them and recommend clinician assessment / secondary prevention rather than calculating a score.

9. Result UX should have FOUR layers:

A. RISK
Large display:
“ความเสี่ยงโรคหัวใจและหลอดเลือดใน 10 ปี”
Example: 12%

B. MEANING
Explain in plain Thai.
Example concept:
“จากข้อมูลที่คุณกรอก คนไทยที่มีลักษณะความเสี่ยงใกล้เคียงกันประมาณ 12 คนจาก 100 คน อาจเกิดโรคหัวใจหรือหลอดเลือดสมองในช่วง 10 ปีข้างหน้า”

Clearly label this as an ESTIMATED population-level risk, not a certainty for an individual.

C. RISK DRIVERS
Show clinically relevant factors that deserve attention, such as:
- elevated SBP
- smoking
- diabetes
- central obesity
- age

Do NOT invent percentage contribution of each factor unless mathematically supported.

D. WHAT TO DO NEXT
Generate general preventive guidance driven by entered risk factors.
Examples:
- elevated BP → repeat BP measurement correctly / evaluate hypertension
- smoking → smoking cessation
- diabetes → optimize diabetes and cardiovascular risk-factor control
- central obesity → weight management, exercise, diet

Do not turn this into autonomous treatment prescribing.

10. RISK CATEGORIES
The percentage risk must be the primary result.

Any category/color label must be:
- a communication layer
- configurable
- stored separately from the equation engine

Do not hard-code treatment decisions from category labels.

11. Privacy:
All calculations must occur CLIENT-SIDE.

Do not transmit or store:
- age
- disease history
- blood pressure
- waist
- calculated risk
or other health inputs on a backend.

Do not use analytics that capture medical form values.

12. References / audit page:
Create a visible section showing:
- original Thai CV Risk source
- validation studies
- current relevant Thai cardiovascular guidelines
- official/reference calculator source
- model version
- date clinically reviewed
